A LITTLE CONTEXT

What are we looking at?

What is a spectrum?

A spectrum is a set of measurements that gives clues about a substance. The position, size, or shape of its peaks can tell a chemist about the molecule’s mass, bonds, or atoms.

What do MS, IR, and NMR mean?

MS (mass spectrometry) gives clues about mass and fragments. IR (infrared spectroscopy) gives clues about bonds. ¹H NMR looks at hydrogen environments; ¹³C NMR looks at carbon environments.

Is this a confirmed identification?

It is a model’s best match within its library. The examples use simulated noisy data, and the percentages are model probabilities, not measured accuracy. Experimental samples need independent confirmation.

Can I use my own files?

For now, keep your spectra beside you and answer the questions from your observations. The tool does not import raw instrument files. The guided example is a good place to learn the workflow first.
