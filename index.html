How the demo works

  1. Pick a compound, or use the caffeine demo. SpecTree loads that compound's spectral reading.
  2. The model chooses the question that best splits its leading candidates, for example “Is there a strong IR band near 1700 cm⁻¹?” or “How many ¹³C signals are there?”
  3. The reading answers the question. Every candidate's probability is updated, and gold arrows on the map trace the order of the questions.
  4. It stops when one structure passes 99.9% probability, after 60 questions, or when no useful question is left. Use Next and Previous to step through each decision.

The readings in this demo are simulated spectra generated for each library compound, not newly measured experimental data. Probabilities are model outputs. Everything runs in your browser; no server or account is needed.
